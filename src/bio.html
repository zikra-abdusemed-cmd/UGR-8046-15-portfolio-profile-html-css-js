Bio

I was born and raised in the walled city of Harar. This city is known to be rich in culture, history, and historical sites, including ancient mosques, markets, and unique architectural designs. Sometimes it is referred to as “The Living Museum.
Harar is a place where every corner carries a sense of discovery and wonder. Growing up there means being immersed in the rhythms of traditional coffee ceremonies, the scent of spices wafting through the air, and the vibrant textiles that color the daily life of its people.

This city is also known for the diversity of cultures and the beauty that comes along with living in harmony.Harar’s diverse influences, blending African, Arab, and Indian cultures, shine through in its cuisine, crafts, and festivals. Harar has earned its reputation as a place where people from different backgrounds come together, fostering a spirit of inclusivity and unity, giving the city another nickname: “city of love.”
